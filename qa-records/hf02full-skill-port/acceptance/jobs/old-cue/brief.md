## 1. The video
- **Product, in one line:** Cue by Manus: your own team of AI agents, each with its own email, phone number and computer, that take real tasks off your hands and only spend money after you approve. *(confirm wording)*
- **Goal:** *open:* the 75 s launch film already exists. Proposed: **a 30 s "what is Cue" explainer for social**, plus a 15 s cutdown and transparent UI clips you can reuse in your own edits.
- **The one thing a viewer should remember:** **Cue doesn't just answer, it gets things done for you.**
- **Call to action:** **Download Cue** (App Store / Google Play buttons + URL) *(confirm stores and URL)*
- **Where it runs → aspects:** **X and LinkedIn (16:9) + Reels/TikTok/Shorts (9:16)**
- **Length:** **30 s main + 15 s cutdown** (one idea every 2–4 s, about 9 shots)
- **Deadline:** *open*

## 2. Words
- **Must say** *(confirm each)*:
  - its own email, phone number and computer
  - buys only after you approve
  - an assistant, not a chatbot
  - a team of agents that splits a job between them
  - works with your apps
- **Must not say** (proposed): no pricing, no comparisons with named competitors, nothing unreleased, no customer names or numbers we can't source.
- **Tone:** **playful-confident**, same family as the launch film. Captions carry the message; no voiceover.

## 6. Script directions — **chosen: B, "A day, handled"** (Alex, 2026-10-20)
**A — "Your own team"** (product tour, the safe choice)
Meet Cue → each agent has its own email, phone and computer → one task end-to-end: prompt, steps tick, approve, booked →
a team splits a trip in a group chat → works with your apps → end card.

**B — "A day, handled"** (story-led, most shareable)
One person's day from 8 am to 8 pm, five tasks crossing off on a clock:
- inbox cleared
- a call handled
- dinner booked (with the approval tap)
- a trip planned by the team
- a pickup ordered

End line: *"Your day. Handled."*

**C — "Ask once"** (UI-centric, doubles as your overlay library)
One big prompt is typed. The agent fans out into a team, steps tick across the screen and one approval tap closes it.
Then a fast montage of three more prompts finishing. Almost every shot is a clean UI moment you can reuse as a clip.
