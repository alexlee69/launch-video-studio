# Saturday — a Cue short (painted, ClaudeAnimationBase)

Logline: Jun wants to throw their best friend a birthday dinner on Saturday, but the week is already full, so Jun hands it
to three Cue agents, who email, call and pay (within budget) in the background, and Jun only has to pick.

World: one desk at night (cold indigo, one warm lamp) → a warm restaurant table on Saturday (amber, candlelight).
Motif: one striped birthday candle. Unlit in Jun's hand in shot A; lit on the cake in shot F.
Cast: Jun (sage sweater, dark bob) · the friend (rose shirt, curls) · three Cue agents, each with one prop that is
its own identity: tri + envelope (its own email), star + phone (its own number), heart + coin purse (its own wallet).
Jun's arc: tired → hopeful → relieved → thinking → happy.  Agents: excited → determined → proud → cheering.
