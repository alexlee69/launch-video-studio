# higgsfield01 — overlay, UI and graphics motion spec (agent C)

**What this is.** The motion design of every overlay, UI and graphics element in `higgsfield01.mp4` (76.53 s, 2296 frames, 1080×608, 30 fps), measured per frame so a builder can animate ours to land on the same frames with the same curves. Study only: none of their copy, art, logos or frames goes into our film.

**Conventions.**
- Frames are 0-based `nNNNN`, matching `ref/frames/`. Positions are px on the 1080×608 reference; the 1920×1080 value (×1.7778) is in square brackets.
- Every number names its tool. "By eye" or "estimated" marks a judgement read off step-1 contact sheets.
- Fits: `tools/fit.py` / `tools/ctfit.py` (cubic-bezier, power, expo, back-out, damped spring), with the max error in px of the measured series. "cf" = one content frame (1/24 s).
- **The film is 24 fps animation in a 30 fps file.** Every frame with `n % 15 ∈ {5, 10, 14}` repeats the previous one (457 of the 459 such frames, film-wide; the exceptions are n2180 and n2189 in the end card; `tables/_film_framediff.json`, checked independently on full-resolution frames). Evaluate curves on content time `t = (n − repeats ≤ n) / 24` to reproduce the source's stepping (`tools/ui_cadence.py`, `tools/ctfit.py`); fits skip the repeats.
- What the brief called the green "Generating…" pill is a 55 px lime Higgsfield icon square followed by plum text, with no pill shape (§3). The mascot's bubble reads "HI, I'M ARA!" (§12).

**Files** (in `overlay/` unless noted):
- This file is `hf01-study/OVERLAY-SPEC.md`, generated by `tools/c_stitch.py` from the lane drafts in `sections/` (edit those, then re-run).
- `tables/`: per-frame JSON. `c-*.json` cover the title, watermark, cat, words and end card; `ui-*.json` the status screens, prompt bars, bubbles and caption; `ae-*.json` the AE screens, cursor, badge, pops and typing. Raw tool outputs are `tables/_*.json`, `tables/raw/`, `tables/track/` and `tables/cursor/`.
- `events.json`: the merged event timeline (also printed in §21).
- `sheets/`: the contact sheets used (title-, wm-, cat-, words-, endcard-, ui-, ae-, check- prefixes).
- `tools/`: `roi.py` (mask bbox per frame), `diff.py`, `fit.py`, `ctfit.py`, `sheet.py`, plus the lane helpers (`ui_*.py`, `ae_*.py`, `wm_*.py`, `words_life.py`, `c_assemble.py`, `c_stitch.py`). Run them with `/usr/bin/python3 -B`.
- `sections/`: the three lanes' drafts this file is stitched from (`c.md`, `ui.md`, `ae.md`, plus each lane's event JSON).

## House grammar (the rules shared across elements)

1. **24 fps steps everywhere.** UI, AE screens, title, cat and watermark all step on source frames; only the end-card background is a true still. Durations of 4–12 cf are the norm.
2. **Hard cut in, already moving.** Nothing cross-fades in. Every screen or card enters on a cut while already moving, then decelerates.
   - **Light screens** enter ≈ 85 px low and rise, `y = 81·e^(−t/0.150 s)` (§3).
   - **AE screens** enter with a rise (expo-out, k ≈ 6), a slide (×0.8 per frame) or a zoom settle (from 0.92–1.13), settled in 4–20 f (§9).
   - **Title pieces** slam in smeared: OPUS 1.6+ → 1.0 in ≈ 9 frames, the pill stretched 2.6× → 1.0 in 5 cf (§1).
3. **Momentum out.** Screens leave on an ease-in that peaks on the frame before the cut.
   - **Rates:** 12–170 px/f or 2–4 %/f; light screens ≈ 17 px/f, upward, with the mirror curve `−89·e^((t−T)/0.150)`.
   - **The title** zooms through, 1 → ≈ 4.7 in 12 cf, about the frame centre.
   - **The only fades in the film:** the watermark, the floating words (rack focus) and the second phone bubble's exit.
4. **One spring family for UI pops; harder back-outs for brand graphics.**
   - **UI:** ζ ≈ 0.5–0.6, ≈ 10 % overshoot at 0.17–0.20 s, settled by ≈ 0.3 s. Examples: the prompt bar (ω 19.5 rad/s, ζ 0.59), the lime icon (ω 23.2, ζ 0.58), the end-card glyph (+10 % then −10 %) and the lockup drop (+21 %, ζ 0.48).
   - **Brand graphics** (AE badges, medallion, pills): back-out over 4–6.5 f, overshooting 13–37 %. Keyframe diamonds use ζ 0.28.
5. **Swap plus ghost, never a morph tween.** Shapes change by a hard swap, often with one ghost or smear frame at ≈ 1.1–1.3×. Examples: AE badge parts, map cards, the cat → block → triangle → fire → code chain, and the code → cat landing (1.27 → 1.00 in 2 cf).
6. **Text moves at three kinds of speed.**
   - **Soft gradient wipe** (status screens, bubbles): 100–190 px/cf (5–9 chars/cf), with a 250–330 px soft edge. Second lines start 2–3 f later.
   - **Hard per-character typing** (prompts, AE):
     - Prompts type 3–4 chars/cf (73–95 chars/s). The first prompt is human-paced: 23–30 chars/s, with 11 f and 17 f pauses.
     - AE display titles type 1–2 letters per source frame, AE body copy ≈ 7.5. A caret shows.
   - **Spoken words** rack-focus in place: ≈ 5 f in, 1–3 f sharp, ≈ 5 f out, 3–4 f apart. They sit on a layer drifting linearly at ≈ 8.3 px/cf.
7. **Never still.** Every hold carries a secondary motion:
   - the title push (+0.26 %/f) and the light screens' drift (≈ 0.6 px/f);
   - chained AE camera moves of 11–20 f;
   - the asterisk rays breathing, shimmer bands and the plum-to-grey drain on the light screens;
   - the prompt outline breathing (≈ 21 f period), the cat floating (±4–5 px) and the words drifting.
8. **Blur only where it is fast.** Directional smear at σ ≈ 0.2 × speed appears on slams, stretches, whips (over ≈ 40 px/f), cut frames and zoom-throughs. Slow settles are crisp.
9. **"Made live" UI.**
   - A selection marquee or handles show for 1–4 f before an element arrives.
   - Photos reveal through a lime pixel-glow ring at 14–30 px per source frame.
   - Signs are placed at 0.3 and drag-resized to 1.0 in 6–8 f.
   - The send button fires an orange pixel burst that sweeps 232 px left in 6 cf.
   - The cursor reads as screen-recorded: it drifts, hops 30–65 px, then holds 3–8 f. Never eased.
10. **Stagger:**
    - List rows and layer bars: 1.75–3 f apart.
    - Words: 3–4 f apart.
    - Pops in a row: ≈ 2.5 f apart.
    - Big cards: ≈ 8–10 f apart, overlapping by about half.
11. **Colour and type.**
    - **Light screens:** #F3F0F5 with near-black or grey grotesk. Claude orange #D37056, lime #CDE321, and plum status text that drains to grey #6B666B.
    - **Coral** #D9785E is the prompt bar's "live" accent, with a burst in #DA7D42.
    - **AE chrome:** #1c1a1f / #1f1c20 / #252227.
    - **Brand comps:** forest green #0b4f2f / #0c452a with sunburst rays; ultra-heavy cream display caps #f6e9d1 with a hard shadow. Accents: lime selection #d3dd49, pink #eb5081, yellow #f7c527.
    - **Mascot:** yellow #e1b730 with a #42231f outline.
    - **Over footage:** white type (title, words, watermark) with soft halos.
12. **The watermark keeps its own clock.** It alternates TR ↔ BL every 270 f (9.0 s): 9 cf fade-out, 11 cf fade-in, on top of everything (§2). The Instagram-gradient sweep plays twice: at n66 on the watermark and at n2187 on the end card.

#### Motion 1: the vertical momentum push (the whole plate)

All the content moves together, vertically only. The tracked dx is ≤ 0.1 px and the text-block width is constant (712–713 px), so there is no scale. The plate enters fast from below, decelerates into a near-hold, then accelerates upward into the exit cut.

- **Fitted model (content time):** `y(t) = A·e^(−t/τ1) + v·t − B·e^((t−T)/τ2) + c`.
  - `t` is seconds since the cut-in frame and `T` is the exit-cut time. `y` is the offset in px from the rest frame; + is down.
  - Fitted with `tools/ui_push.py` (Nelder-Mead) on the NCC plate tracks.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| s2 | n928 → n973 (T 1.500) | 80.6 | 0.149 | 86.9 | 0.146 | −4.4 | 5.1 | 0.61 px | 0.30 |

- **House recipe** (one shared curve; only `c` varies per screen): `y(t) = 81·e^(−t/0.150) − 89·e^((t−T)/0.150) + c`. Its max error is 2.7 px on s1/s2 and 0.9 px on s3/s4. In 1920 px that is 144 px and 158 px.
- **Speeds:**
  - **In:** the first visible frame sits ~85 px [151] below rest and moves up at ~29 px/frame (s1: n661→662 is 91→62 px).
  - **Hold:** at the slowest point the plate drifts ~0.6–0.7 px/frame. That point is n684, n954, n1284 and n1384 (k ≈ 23–26 frames after the cut).
  - **Out:** the plate leaves at ~17 px/frame, reaching about −65 px on the last frame before the cut.
- **Blur:** strong vertical motion blur on the first frame (the asterisk at n661 smears ~25 px tall), lighter on the next 2, and again on the last 2 frames (n696–697, n971–972 …). Estimated σy ≈ 0.2 × speed.

**Duration:** 37 frames for s1, s3 and s4; 45 for s2. The text reveals ride on top of the push.

#### Motion 2: the text reveal (a soft left-to-right wipe, not a type-on)

Each line appears through a wide, soft gradient front moving left to right. Glyphs fade in as the front passes; there is no caret and no per-character pop.

- **Method:** `ui_reveal.py`. For each line, the ink in a 16 px window is compared with the fully revealed rest frame, which is shifted by the plate's dy.

  - **soft edge** = x(ratio ≥ 0.1) − x(ratio ≥ 0.9).
