# The Lull vs higgsfield01, beat by beat

**FINAL storyboard v1.0 (astronaut; comedy base + the three judges' grafts), 2026-10-23, storyboard only, for Alex's review.** Same 27 shots, 26 cuts and five send frames as hf01 (n652 / 913 / 1181 / 1641 / 1895); our story, copy and plates. hf01's lines are quoted only to compare the beats.

| Beat | higgsfield01 | Ours (The Lull) | Why ours holds up |

**Where we are weaker, said plainly.** hf01 has a mascot with personality (the cat) and we have none, because the Manus mark is never a character: her deadpan face and the zero-G props carry that job. hf01's prompts are plain briefs; ours are jokes, which means each one must land in about a second of reading. Our plan also costs more to generate (12 live plates in space, one face through a helmet bubble in 8 of them).
