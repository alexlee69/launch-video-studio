# W1 — open, fly-through, outro, end card

**Status: built and measured; waiting for the go to re-lay out.** Our render was tracked with the same tools as the reference. Every cut lands on the reference's frame, and every measured peak is within ±0.5 f, except the corridor items flagged below.

The assets are ours: the mark, lockup, badge, hall art and crew, with copy from `story.js`. **The layouts are not yet ours.** They still follow the reference's arrangement:
- a centred two-part lockup with a pill below, crew scattered around it;
- a crew ring flying down a hall;
- a centred badge with the handle under it.

I only moved things around inside that structure: a hexagon scatter instead of 3 + 3, and the hall tracks rotated −30°. Per the integrator's note I stopped composition work there. Every curve below is written so it can be re-applied to a new layout.

## What is approximate, and why

- **n75 and n76 camera scale.** zoomfit can't fit the reference's n75 (ncc .47). 5.25 is read from the join mark plus the expo-in continuation; n76 = 10.9 continues it, and only blurred remnants show. Ours tracks 5.35 at n75.
- **Shutter.** The reference's blur isn't one shutter: .20–.24 on the zoom, σ/v .05–.10 on the slide (strongest at the peak). One 0.22 shutter matches the peaks; at low speeds (slide n16–17) ours is slightly blurrier.
- **Hall flow.**
  - tunnel.py fits a single elliptical section. ncc is .5–.93, and the n86–90 and n92–94 pairs are unreliable on both clips (big characters, a white end wall).
  - `gain` and the +5 % on k(83.5) and k(84.5) calibrate our octagonal hall to read like the reference with the same tool. They are calibration, not new motion.
- **Hall crew.** n76–79 and n95–96 positions and sizes were read by eye from zoomed crops. The square and triangle n80–94 come from a template tracker with patchy ncc. n96 positions are off-frame estimates.
- **Grey-square character.** Its pop start (n9.9), pulse (n24, ×0.8) and converge tail were read from crops; the blob tool can't see light grey on white.
- **Crew float.** The gentle ±1.6 / ±2.2 px float on the lockup crew is ours, not measured. The reference's characters drift with the camera, and on their own by < 0.3 px/f.
- **End-card spin.** The rate was estimated from which quadrant holds the warmest hue (TL n1812 → TR ~n1834 → BR ~n1856, ≈ 4.1°/f clockwise).
- **Outro crew spread.** The 12 % spread is a composition estimate (blob positions, outro vs opening).

## INTEGRATOR

1. **Z order around the W1→W2 handoff.** It works as built:
   - `w1-corridor` z 8 and `w1-crew-in` z 10, n76–94.
   - `w2-plate1` z 70 from n95. Its background is transparent at n95, and the hall shows around the card.

   - From n96 the hall's end wall is paper (#F5F3EF) and fills the frame.

   If W2 makes n95 opaque, the hall's last frame disappears.
2. **`tools/qa.py` on a range that includes n1787–1908 will always FAIL.** That's the deliberate black frame and the 20 fps duplicates, as in the reference. Whitelist n1787 and the end card's repeats, or check that range by eye.
3. **`tools/cutcheck.py` reports n75 as +4 f.** It's a content false alarm; see above.
4. **The tracking tools** (`track.py`, `cutcheck.py`, my `tunnel.py`/`zoomfit.py`) assume 1080-wide frames. Our renders are 1920×1081. `tools/w1/down1080.py <src> <dst>` makes the 1080 copies; a resize inside `track.py` would save every builder this step.
5. **Story change (tagline 'Six agents, one studio').** It fits: the pill sizes itself to the text. The type-on keeps the reference's start and end frames, so it types faster (3.8 chars/f in the open, 1.3 in the outro, against 2.7 and 0.94).
6. **Rendering note for everyone.** A DOM `preserve-3d` hall in 14 averaged copies made Chrome drop layers at DSF 1.78: random missing characters and walls. The hall is now a single canvas with its own projection and sub-frame accumulation. Keep big 3D DOM stacks small.
7. **Layout.** Per the integrator's note, I've stopped matching the reference's compositions and haven't re-laid anything out yet. Three things in `scenes/w1-open.js` are layout parameters, not motion:
   - the `layout()` slots (rest and target per character);
   - the lockup geometry;
   - `corr.rot`.

   The motion tables don't depend on them. On a new layout:
   - keep the camera pivots (546, 300) for the push and (539, 309) for the outro, or move them with the new focal point;
   - put the join or focal point where the push should dive;
   - re-aim the converge targets.
