## 1. Conventions (read first)

- **Space and time.** Units are reference px in the 1080×608 frame (the stage renders them at 1920×1080). n is the reference frame at 30 fps; fractional keys are allowed. Tables are `{frame: value}`, piecewise-linear (`trk`), held flat outside their range. `vel(t, n) = trk(n+½) − trk(n−½)`, in units per frame.
- **Camera tables (`tx`, `ty`).** They give where the shot's *key-frame layout* sits in frame n: a point at (x, y) in the key layout appears at (x+tx, y+ty). Apply them as a pure translation of the whole shot (`S.camera(tx, ty)`).
  - **No zoom anywhere in W3's ranges.** Tracker scale stays within ±0.2 % in every shot; the reference reframes by cutting, not by zooming.
  - **Key frames**: A n300 (A0 is re-based to it), B n345, D n470, R n695, G n732, I n987. C has no camera; its elements carry absolute frame-y tables.
- **Blur** is a Gaussian σ in px, x and y separately. "Scene blur" means the whole shot (`S.blur`); otherwise it's on the named element.
  - **House rule for motion blur**: σ = k·|v| along the motion. Measured k:
    - 0.20 in most shots;
    - 0.17 in the big type (applied as 0.84 × 0.2);
    - ≈0.2 on the insights entrance;
    - 0.22 for the roster list and characters, minus 0.4 px.
  - **Cut frames.** The tracker over-reads blur ~2× where other elements fade in on those frames. The B values are already calibrated for that.
- **Element offset tables** (`replyRise`, `nameGlide`, `textRise`, `lineRise`, `upLead`, `header`) are px added to the element's rest position *on top of* the camera. Positive y = below rest; negative x = left of rest.
- **"Ink" tables** (`nameInk`, `up0`, `up1`) are blur-invariant darkness weights, normalised to the finished state. Apply them as colour = mix(background, final colour, w).
  - Opacity tables (`…Op`, `items`, `nums`, `chipPop`) are opacity.
  - `up0`/`up1` are multipliers on a base darkness (see G).
- **"Frac" tables** (`statusFrac`) are the measured ink fraction of a finished label being wiped in left → right. Apply them by inverting the soft wipe's covered fraction (`wipeFrac(node, f, edge)` in the scene file; edge 0.4, or 0.6 for R).
  - "Wipe" tables from the earlier study (`B.placeWipe`, `D.statusWipe`, `D.status2Wipe`) are the wipe parameter p itself (`wipe(node, p, 0.4)`).
- **Reveal specs `{t0, rate, D, k, n}`** describe text that types or streams in.
  - Character i (0-based) starts at t0 + i/rate′. Its opacity is 1 − (1 − u)^k, where u = clamp((frame − start)/D).
  - n is the *reference* line's character count. rate′ = rate·(N − 1)/(n − 1) for our line of N characters, so a line of any length starts on t0 and its last character starts when the reference's did (`reveal()` in the scene file).
  - The measured rates (chars/frame) are the `rate` values.
- **Pivots for scale**:
  - send button: its centre;
  - character pop: the body centre (the reference blob's centroid stays fixed during the pop, to 0.1 px);
  - grid dots: centre;
  - our status chip: left edge, vertical centre.
