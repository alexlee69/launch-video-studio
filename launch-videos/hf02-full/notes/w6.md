- entries blur with the forward step, exits with the backward one;
- the edge-fit σ at n1262–1264 and n1276–1279 matches that rule.

Corrections and exceptions:
- **Split entry, n1261–1266:** the reference reads ~20 % above the rule, so the factor there is 0.24.

- **The phone:** never blurs.

## Approximate, and why

- **Split, n1261 and n1280.** These are the cut-adjacent readings: d 34 (reference) against 36 (ours) at n1261. The reference's n1280 reading moves by 8 px between two edge methods because of heavy blur on a pale square. Our n1280 left-edge σ reads 15.8 against 7.1 because our Writer's highlight gives a two-step edge profile; it is visibly the same blur in `cmp_cuts_a.png`.
- **Burst rest angles are ours, not the reference's.** Radii, sizes, counts and per-group curves are measured. The burst 1 inner pair is merged on the reference until n1287, so n1281–1286 are extrapolated (×1.75/f), capped at −17 px.
- **Cluster n1317–1323.** R and S were fitted on whole-frame moments, not per character; they overlap there.
- **Bubble fly-in path, n1480–1493.** Read by eye on crops; ±3 px over the phone and ±10 px over paper, where the blurred white character is faint. The pill rows and growth were measured by tool.
- **The list rise curve** is the reference's first-card top edge, scaled ×1.43 so our shorter rows also enter from the frame's bottom edge at n1472. The rows are staggered 2 f, like the reference's second card. The tilt (−16°) is eyeballed.
- **Screen fades** come from the card's interior luminance (n1373–1383 and n1416–1428). The library settle (14 px, n1443–1449) and the tap ring (n1468–1469) come from per-frame sheets.
- **The chat whip after n1421** is extrapolated: the text is off-frame by n1424, as on the reference.

## INTEGRATOR

1. **`tools/track.py` reads frames as-is.** Our renders are 1920×1081, so ty and tx on our own frames come out ×1.78. `cmp.py` and the others resize. Workaround: `tools/w6/to1080.py` writes 1080-wide copies first. Suggest `track.py` crop and resize the way `cmp.py` does.
2. **`mountScenes()` hides each layer *before* calling `build()`,** so `getBoundingClientRect()` returns 0 during build. That breaks `agentLine()` (its status lands on the name) and any text measuring. Workaround: `w6-app` sets `S.root.style.display = 'block'` during build and restores it. Suggest hiding after build.
3. **z order.** My scenes are z 60. W2's `w2-plate2` (z 70, from n1529) is correctly above my phone at n1529–1531, and they don't overlap on screen. The W5→W6 cut at n1259/1260 has no shared frame; W5's Builder flies in and W6 opens on the Builder close-up.
4. **Frozen frames n1450–1463 are intentional** (the reference holds there). If film QA must have zero frozen frames, I can add an invisible 0.1 px/f drift. Say so.
5. **`hush()` screens are drawn at 180:390.** My phone uses the reference's 0.563 aspect, so `hushFit()` lifts the tab bar and hides it on the player. A `hush(..., {h})` that re-flows would help anyone else who needs a taller-than-wide phone.
6. **No new story lines needed.** The player labels reuse `STORY.chat.insights.items[2]`. The Integrator's copy revision has been handled (see *What is ours*).
