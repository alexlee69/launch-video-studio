When building a deliverable Alex will circulate (e.g. the Oct 2 event PPT), send the first complete, validated draft as soon as it exists, then keep polishing and send a versioned update (v0.1 → v0.2).

**Why:** 2026-09-28, mid-build: "give me the ppt first then i will share with the team and collect feedback" — team feedback is the real gate; QA polish can land in the next version.

**How to apply:** gate the early send on hard checks only (file validates, all content present, nothing broken on a contact-sheet look), label it DRAFT vX, list what is still being polished and what needs sign-off, and version later sends so feedback maps to a file.
